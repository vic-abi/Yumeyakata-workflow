Reference notes

Required booking info

  • Guest name
  • No. of guests (Men / Ladies / Boys / Girls)
  • Height (for kimono sizing)
  • Return method (in-store or hotel FD)
  • Selected add-ons (hair / makeup / upgrade)

Add-ons (selected at booking)

  • Standard hairstyling (ladies) ¥1,650
  • Full makeup (ladies) ¥4,400
  • Upgrade to premium kimono from ¥6,820
All add-ons paid at Yumeyakata on kimono day. Prices subject to change.

Payment flow

  • Route ①: pay now (room + kimono) or pay later
  • Route ②: pay later, ideally 3 days before
  • Minimum: 1 day before, no same-day bookings
  • All add-ons: paid at Yumeyakata on the day
  • Hotel FD return fee (¥550): pre-paid at Yumeyakata
Reminder emails sent 3 days before the experience

Return options

  • At Yumeyakata: free, until 17:30, daily pickup
  • At hotel front desk: ¥550, pre-paid at Yumeyakata on the day
  • Hotel FD return hours: TBC, to confirm with Yumeyakata
No prices shown on the hotel side. All payments at Yumeyakata directly.
